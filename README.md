## Overview
Our app is a simple, accessible campsite reservation application that replaces the race to refresh a booking page with a fair lottery. Campsite owners can register their campsites, upload photos, set their location on a map, and open a lottery for a block of dates, with a unique QR code that links to the listing. Entrants can search and filter campsites by location, date, and number of guests, or scan a poster’s QR code, then join the waiting list with a single tap. When the registration period ends, the system automatically draws the winners and notifies them, and winners can choose their dates and reserve, or decline and let someone else have the spot. Entrants can track their active and past reservations, and owners can monitor waiting lists and bookings and reject a reservation if something comes up. Administrators moderate campsites, profiles, and images so the community stays safe and trustworthy.

## Features
* Registering/updating/removing campsites, with details, tags, a map location, and an optional promo image
* Unique QR code generated for each campsite that links straight to its listing
* Creating/updating lotteries for one or more campsites, with registration and booking periods, number of winners, and an optional waitlist capacity
* Searching campsites and filtering by location, date, and number of guests
* Joining or leaving a lottery waiting list from the campsite details screen or by scanning a QR code
* Automatic lottery draw when the registration period ends, with win and lose notifications
* Choosing dates (and campsites, for multi-campsite lotteries) when you win, or declining to pass the spot on
* Policy and rules confirmation before a reservation is finalized
* Viewing active, past, and cancelled reservations, with cancellation and rejection support
* Reservation confirmations by notification or downloadable CSV
* Campsite ratings and an availability calendar for owners
* Device-based sign-in, so there are no usernames or passwords to manage
* Admin moderation of campsites, profiles, images, and notification and message logs
* Simple and accessible UI design
